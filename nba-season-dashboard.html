Methodology note

Odds are preseason opening or early-October lines and shift constantly once games tip off — treat every figure here as a snapshot, not a live line. Player stat lines are final 2025-26 regular season per-game averages, the most recent complete season available. The 2026-27 season itself had not generated meaningful statistics at the time this dashboard was built.

The record this season's opener has to beat

The most recent landmark came in 2024: the defending-champion Celtics' ring night. Boston drained 29 three-pointers — a new NBA single-game record for any game, opener or not — after becoming the first team ever to hit 10 threes in the opening quarter of a season. It shattered the previous opening-night 3PT record of 23, set by the Hornets in 2019.

07Does the best regular season actually win the title?

Ten champions, ten different win totals — anywhere from 46 to 68. The 2024-25 Thunder put up the most regular-season wins by an eventual champion since the 1996-97 Bulls. There's no fixed number a team needs; there's a wide band, and recent years have trended toward the higher end of it.
