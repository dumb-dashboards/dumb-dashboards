CH.01The word that survives every record

A corpus-linguistics thesis on Julian's lyrics names "wait" as his most frequent word overall. In your own per-album counts, "wait" shows up in only 3 of 7 records and never ranks #1. "know" is in the top-12 of all seven albums, no exceptions — the only word with that record. The trace below plots its frequency normalized against each album's word count (not the raw count — the records aren't the same length).

TRACE 01 · "KNOW" — % OF WORDS ANALYZED

Lowest point: Reality Awaits (0.85%) — never this quiet in 25 years. Highest point: Comedown Machine (3.8%) — but it's also the album with the smallest word count in the whole set (445), so every word carries more weight there.

CH.04The "Adults Are Talking" anomaly

It's not the most-played song live (101 plays), nor the top critic pick — but on TikTok the sound has crossed 161,000 user clips, more than any other Strokes song. It also shares the highest community score on Album of the Year (96/100), tied with Ode to the Mets and Reptilia.

CH.05Scores, record by record

The strangest gap: Reality Awaits is the first record where fans (53) land far below critics (67) — a 14-point spread, the widest in the whole catalog and the only case running "critics above fans." The New Abnormal has the biggest gap in the other direction: +11 in favor of fans.

CH.09Reality Awaits — record #7

Released: Jul 24, 2026 (pushed back from June)
Producer: Rick Rubin, recorded in Costa Rica
Runtime: 41:02 · 9 tracks
Critic score (AOTY): 67/100 · Fans: 53/100
Live plays 31 Words 939

Recorded with Rick Rubin — the same producer as The New Abnormal — in a house on a Costa Rican mountaintop, which Rubin described as "a concert for the ocean." Julian leans hard on AutoTune throughout, deliberately: critics flagged it almost unanimously, for better (intentional alienation) and worse (it buries some melodies). Recurring themes: corruption, aging, nostalgia, and what's left of an already-cemented legacy.
