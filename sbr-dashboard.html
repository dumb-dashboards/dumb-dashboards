06The race would never clear a permit office today

Modern requirementWhy the Steel Ball Run fails it
Interstate event permittingNo single agency could authorize an unregulated 6,000km course crossing 9+ states with zero road-closure coordination
Horse Protection Act (1970)Federal law banning cruel treatment of competition horses — an open-ended, unsupervised, multi-day endurance race would violate it almost immediately
General liability insuranceNo insurer would underwrite an event with a predictable serious-injury rate this high; without it, most US venues and roads legally can't host it
State/federal gambling lawA $60M winner-take-most purse funded partly by wagering would trigger state gaming commission oversight almost everywhere
Visa & immigration lawOpen, no-visa entry for competitors from Italy, Mexico, India and elsewhere isn't how any nation's borders work today
Firearms across state linesCompetitors carrying guns through 9+ states hits a patchwork of conflicting state carry laws the moment they cross a border

None of this requires knowing a single plot detail — it's just what happens when a 19th-century wild-west race meets 21st-century regulation.

Stage 1: already a liability nightmare

An unmarked course with cacti and terrain hazards, no course marshals, no medical staff on standby — Avdul's elimination (riding straight into a cactus grove mid-race) is exactly the kind of foreseeable, preventable injury that ends careers in real regulatory law. Any US racing commission would have shut the course down at the first inspection.
