Calendar FM

Songs that work one day a year. How much does a single day beat a normal one?

Read this first. The curves come from Google Trends: they measure search interest, not streams. Every song is scaled to its own best week (100), so songs are never compared by size, only by how much their big day beats their normal week. Weekly data, worldwide, Nov 2021 to Nov 2026.

The Situational Index

Peak week divided by a typical week (the median week of the series). Bigger means the song depends more on its one day. The bar is on a log scale.

The plot twist

Mariah is searched less every year, and streamed more every year.

The Year in Motion

Every curve drawn from January to December. It is the typical year: for each week we take the median over the years we have. Log scale, so the small songs stay visible.

The Heat Calendar

52 weeks, 7 songs. Darker means more search interest than a normal week for that song (log scale).

The Heartbeat

Five years of weekly interest for each song, each on its own scale. A flat line, then one spike a year.
