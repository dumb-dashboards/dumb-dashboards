02Love On Tour: real numbers, real scale

169 shows, $617.3M gross, 5M tickets — among the very highest-grossing tours in Billboard Boxscore's 30+ year history. "Together, Together" hasn't finished its run yet, so a final gross isn't calculable — but industry estimates put its per-show grosses 25–30% above Love On Tour's rate, thanks to fewer shows and much higher per-city demand.

Source: Billboard Boxscore, via Billboard.com reporting (2023, 2026).

Same tour, wildly different ticket prices

Love On Tour's average ticket sat at $122.89 — but that hides a huge spread: New York ran nearly 3.5x Mexico City's price for the same tour, same setlist.

Seven songs, seven separate billion-stream milestones

"Golden" became his 7th song to cross 1 billion Spotify streams in mid-2025. "Sign of the Times" (2017) only crossed 2 billion in early 2026 — nine years after release, streams keep compounding.

Source: ChartMasters, TikTok music-news aggregation citing Spotify data.

Love On Tour (2021–23) was the classic model: 169 shows spread across dozens of cities on 5 continents. Together, Together inverts that — far fewer cities, each one a multi-week residency instead of a single stop.

Source: MSG Entertainment press release; Time Out London; Billboard.

The show itself: five acts, twenty songs

Roughly half the current setlist is new material from "Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally." (2026) — a real, current-tour-specific split, not a historical average. The show is structured in five distinct segments: Act I (strings, ending on "Fine Line"), Act II, an X-Stage run, an A-Stage run, then a three-song encore closing on "Matilda," "Sign of the Times," and "As It Was."

Source: Time Out London (Wembley), Capital FM (MSG setlist breakdown), Billboard (opening night, Amsterdam).

For context: when a fan tradition went wrong (previous tour)

Nov 2023, Kia Forum (Inglewood) — Love On Tour, not the current tour: mid-"Kiwi," Styles was hit in the eye with a handful of Skittles thrown from the crowd, hard enough that he kept the eye closed for the rest of the song. He finished the show; his band later confirmed he was okay.

Source: Billboard, Nov 2023.

Methodology note

Setlist and show-timing statistics for the current tour are sourced from live concert coverage (Time Out, Capital FM, Billboard) rather than aggregated setlist.fm career averages, so they reflect Together, Together specifically. Career-wide averages (show length, total distinct songs) are still setlist.fm community-logged data and reflect attendee reporting, not official tour data. Streaming figures are platform-reported (Spotify) and change daily; figures here are snapshots from the cited dates. Box office figures are as reported to Billboard Boxscore, but 2026–27 tour figures for "Together, Together" are partial/in-progress since the tour is still running.
