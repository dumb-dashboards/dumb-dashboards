The Bounty Board

29 years, 600M+ copies, and a captain whose bounty went from 30 million to 3 billion berries. Every chart below, counted and cross-checked by hand.
⚠ SPOILER CUTOFF: everything here stops at Viz Media Volume 111 (Wano → early Egghead). Nothing from the Elbaph arc or beyond. If Japan is ahead of you, you're safe here too.

01The best-selling comic ever made by one person

RECORD

600 million+ copies in circulation worldwide (450M in Japan, 150M+ overseas) — a Guinness World Record for "most copies published for the same comic book series by a single author." Volume 114 alone crossed 1 million copies in Japan within a month of release in March 2026, the only manga to do that in 2026 so far.

Source: Shueisha (obi-strip announcement), Guinness World Records, GameRant.

29 years, one story

Chapters serialized since July 1997. The anime just cut its own pace too — from 30+ episodes/year down to a 26/year cap, announced in 2026.

Live-action, at actual scale

Two Netflix seasons so far: 15.5 hours total. The anime alone is over 1,150 episodes — roughly 290+ times longer.

The longest sagas, by episode count

Wano alone runs 197 episodes — more than Zou, Marineford, and Skypiea combined. Dressrosa is the runner-up, and famously contains zero filler despite the length.

02Luffy's bounty, all seven of them

WANTED

Log scale — the jump from 30M to 3B doesn't fit any other way. Flat stretches (Skypiea, Thriller Bark) mean the world simply didn't find out about anything new.

The whole crew, post-Wano

Chopper's bounty doubled after Wano — from 500 to 1,000 berries. He remains, canonically, the least-threatening 3-billion-berry captain's crewmate in the world.

How scary the final boss got, arc by arc

Using each arc's main antagonist bounty as a rough power proxy. Arlong (East Blue) to Kaido (Wano): a 230,000x increase across the story so far.

Who Luffy actually fights, by category

Independent pirate captains (Buggy, Kuro, Krieg, Arlong, Hordy Jones) make up most of his fight record by sheer count. The higher tiers — Warlords, government agents, Yonko-crew executives, and a Yonko himself — come later and far less often, which is exactly the point.

Luffy's transformations, deep dive

GEAR UP
Gear 2nd & 3rd — Enies Lobby ('07)

Does: Gear 2 pumps blood like a turbocharger for a hard spike in speed and power. Gear 3 inflates his bones for a giant-sized bruiser mode.

Catch: Gear 2 burns stamina fast and canonically shortens his life a little each use. Gear 3 shrinks him to a small child right after.

Gear 4th — Dressrosa ('16)

Does: Haki + rubber armor hybrid, with sub-forms (Boundman, Tankman, Snakeman) for different fights.

Catch: Needs recovery time afterward — can't chain it back-to-back.

Gear 5th — Wano ('22)

Does: Awakens his fruit's true identity — cartoon-physics, reality-bending power.

Catch: Long-term cost still unknown; visibly exhausts him harder than any Gear before it.

Real-world years between each unlock: 9 years from Gear 2/3 to Gear 4, then only 6 years to Gear 5 — the power-ups are arriving faster, not slower, as the story nears its end.

04The running gags, greatest hits

No wiki keeps an official tally of these, so instead of inventing a fake precise count, here are the standout moments — curated, not counted.

Sanji's nosebleeds

His worst-ever recorded case: seeing a mermaid on Fish-Man Island triggers a nosebleed so severe it's described as nearly life-threatening, with blood spraying in the shape of a mermaid. Doctor's orders afterward: stay away from beautiful women. It doesn't take.

Chapter 609, Fish-Man Island Arc
Usopp's lies that came true

"I have giant friends" — years before anyone believed him, he actually did (Dorry and Brogy). "There's a floating island in the sky" — Skypiea turned out to be real too. Nearly every tall tale he's told has eventually happened.

Running motif since East Blue
Zoro vs. Sanji, the nicknames

Zoro calls him "ero-cook" and "Prince of Dumbass Kingdom." Sanji calls him "marimo" (moss-head). They argue constantly, fight side by side without hesitation, and would each deny respecting the other under torture.

Ongoing since crew formation

Luffy's meat consumption, very roughly

This one is not sourced — nobody has ever tallied it. It's our own napkin math: story time spans roughly 3 in-universe years; assume Luffy eats meat at nearly every meal, several servings at a time. That's the whole methodology. Treat it as a joke with a bar chart, not a fact.

Devil Fruit users on the crew

Half the ten-person crew has eaten a Devil Fruit. The other half gets by on swordsmanship, cooking, navigation, and one very well-built ship.
