The gaps between numbered entries

KH1 to KH2: 3 years. KH2 to KH3: 13 years — one of the longest waits for a numbered sequel in gaming history. KH3 to KH4: 8 years and counting, landing right on the franchise's 25th anniversary.

What we know about KH4 so far

  • Announced for Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC — day-one on every major platform
  • Set in Quadratum, a realistic city Sora wakes up in, unable to find his way home
  • Confirmed Disney world: Pixar's Coco (2017), with actor Benjamin Bratt reprising his role
  • Kicks off what Square Enix calls the "Lost Master Arc" — a new saga after the Dark Seeker Saga's conclusion in KH3
  • An original anime series is also in development for Disney+ and Disney Channel

Source: D23 Disney Entertainment Showcase, August 14, 2026 (Square Enix / Disney press materials).

Where the worlds actually come from

Original, KH-only locations (Traverse Town, Twilight Town, Radiant Garden, and more) outnumber any single Disney era. Pixar didn't show up at all until 2019's KH3 — 17 years into the franchise.

03Metacritic, every mainline entry

The highest and lowest scores in the franchise are 21 points apart, and the worst-reviewed entry (Re:coded, 66) started life as a Japan-only phone game before anyone in the West ever played it.

The pattern nobody wants to admit

The two highest-rated games in the entire franchise are also the only two with the simplest titles: Kingdom Hearts (85) and Kingdom Hearts II (87). The more baroque the subtitle gets, the further the score tends to drift from that peak — not a strict rule, but close enough to notice.

04A whole game got cancelled after 3 years of hype

Kingdom Hearts Missing-Link was announced in April 2022 alongside KH4 itself, ran as a mobile closed beta for years, built a real community — and was quietly cancelled in May 2025 with no game ever fully released. It is, technically, one of the 34 official entries, and also one that almost nobody ever got to finish.

Of the 13 core members of the series' central villain group, only the leader (Xemnas) and his second (Xigbar) stay loyal to the very end. Everyone else defects, dies fighting it, or switches sides — an 85% desertion rate from an organization literally built on shared purpose.

Curated nonsense from the franchise, for the record

A title with a decimal point in it

"Kingdom Hearts 0.2 Birth by Sleep –A Fragmentary Passage–" is a real, shipped, reviewed (66 on Metacritic) video game. It is treated as a completely normal entry by the numbering system.

A slash in the title, on purpose

"358/2 Days" isn't a typo or a fraction — it refers to 358 in-game days over 2 playable characters. Nobody who hasn't looked this up has ever correctly guessed why it's named that.

A Greek letter, also on purpose

"Kingdom Hearts χ [chi]" is pronounced "Kingdom Hearts Union Cross." The letter is silent, present, and doing absolutely nothing to help newcomers.

The community's own coping mechanism

Nearly every fan wiki, Steam guide, and YouTube video about "how to play Kingdom Hearts" opens by apologizing to the reader before the list even starts. This dashboard is not exempt.
