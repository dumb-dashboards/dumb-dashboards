WHAT IS A RANDOM EVENT?

While you drive around San Andreas, short street scenes pop up on the map. A blue dot means someone needs help, a red dot means someone needs stopping. You can help, ignore it, or make it worse.

Methodology and sources

Vehicle counts, classes, types and update packs come from FiveForge (read from game files); other counts are quoted from each source's own definition. Map sizes are deliberately absent: Rockstar has never published them and every figure online is a fan measurement. Brand and website lookalikes are fan-assigned. Trailer view records are company claims. Nothing here uses leaked or hacked data.
