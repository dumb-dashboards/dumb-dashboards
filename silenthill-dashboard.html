SILENT HILL
Case File

25 years, 17.1 million copies, a 12-year silence, and a joke ending about aliens hiding in 8 out of 10 games. A full audit ahead of Townfall (Sept 24, 2026).

01Twenty-five years, one long silence

Franchise sales 17.1M
First game 1999
The gap 12 years
Townfall releases Sept 24, 2026

Between Book of Memories (2012) and the revival starting with The Short Message (2024), Silent Hill went completely dark for 12 years — longer than the gap between any two Kingdom Hearts games. Since then: three games in three years.

Source: Konami investor sales report (March 2026); Wikipedia release timeline.

Metacritic, every mainline entry

The 2001 original remains the highest-rated entry in the franchise at 89 — the 2024 remake, made 23 years later with modern technology and a bigger budget, still didn't beat it.

David and a much, much bigger Goliath

Resident Evil launched only 3 years before Silent Hill (1996 vs. 1999) — and has sold over 11x as many copies since. One single Resident Evil game, RE5 alone (19M), comes close to outselling Silent Hill's entire 25-year, ~10-game catalog combined.

Source: Capcom investor announcements (200M+ as of May 2026); Konami sales report (Silent Hill, March 2026).

Same genre, very different metabolism

RESIDENT EVIL

Latest entry (Requiem, Feb 2026) sold 5M copies in under a week — the fastest in franchise history. Never had a gap longer than a few years.

SILENT HILL

Fastest-selling entry (Silent Hill f) took 6 months to reach 2M. Survived a 12-year gap where the franchise nearly vanished entirely.

02The full series, in hours

Every mainline game's main-story length, plus the series total: roughly 76.5 hours to see every main story once, 137 hours to see every game 100% — joke endings included.

Source: HowLongToBeat, aggregated via TheGamer.

"Eight to ten hours of game play for $69!?"

That's an actual Silent Hill f player, on the game's own Steam discussion board, doing the same price-per-hour math you'd do for anything else. Silent Hill f launched at $69.99 for an 8–10 hour main story — among the shortest entries in the franchise, at the franchise's highest-ever launch price.

Source: Steam Community discussions, SILENT HILL f General Discussions.

The weirdest weapons in the fog

The chainsaw does a Leatherface dance

Stand idle with it equipped near an enemy and the protagonist lifts it overhead exactly like Leatherface from Texas Chainsaw Massacre — a direct, deliberate reference (the in-game shop is even called "Cut Rite Chainsaws").

The best weapons got cut from the remake

Pyramid Head's massive "Great Knife" and the reality-warping "Hyper Spray" were fan-favorite unlockables in the 2001 original. Neither made it into the 2024 remake.

Officially, the worst weapon in the series

Silent Hill 4's paper cutting knife is described in-game as "hard to use as a weapon" and is widely regarded by fans as the single worst weapon across the whole franchise — worse even than the bug spray in the same game.

Source: Silent Hill Wiki (Fandom); TheGamer weapon guides.

03The aliens show up in 8 out of 10 games

A joke "UFO ending" is a running tradition across nearly the entire franchise — present in Silent Hill 1, 2, 3, Origins, Homecoming, Shattered Memories, The Arcade, and (very likely) f. Only Silent Hill 4 and Downpour skip it — and Downpour swaps in its own different joke ending instead.

Source: Silent Hill Wiki (Fandom), "UFO Ending" article.

Silent Hill 2 alone has 8 endings

Six count as "real" narrative conclusions (Leave, In Water, Maria, Rebirth, and the remake's new Bliss and Stillness). Two — Dog and UFO — are jokes the developers built entire unlock sequences around.

Every ending across 6 games, by tone

19 endings, hand-classified by what actually happens in each one — not a third-party ranking. Positive/hopeful: SH1 Good/Good+, SH2 Leave, Origins Good, Shattered Memories' Broken. Almost exactly a third of the franchise's endings are jokes.
